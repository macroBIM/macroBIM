This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit macroBIM Engineering (macrobim.blogspot.com, "this blog"), how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information collected

SourceWhat is collected
Blogger (Google)This blog is hosted on Google's Blogger platform. Google automatically receives standard log data such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited and referring site, and Blogger provides the author with aggregated, anonymous page-view statistics.
Contact formIf you send a message, the name, email address and message you enter are delivered to the author by email. They are used only to reply to you and are not sold or shared.
CommentsIf comments are enabled and you post one, your comment and the Google profile name you post with are public.
Calculators and downloadsThe calculators, quizzes and DXF downloads on this blog run entirely in your browser. The values you enter are not sent to or stored by this blog.
Third-party contentSome pages load fonts from Google Fonts and charting code from the Plotly content delivery network (cdn.plot.ly). Images are served from GitHub Pages. These services may receive your IP address and browser information when your browser requests the files.

Cookies and advertising

This blog may display advertisements served by Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • To learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where required by law (for example for visitors in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom), Google shows a consent message before personalized ads are served. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the blog will still work, but some ads may be less relevant.

Children's information

This blog is written for engineers and engineering students and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can browse every article without giving any personal information. If you have sent a message through the contact form and want it deleted, ask through the same form and I will delete it.

This policy may be updated when the blog's services change. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
